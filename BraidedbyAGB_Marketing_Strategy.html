01

Where You Are Right Now

Before mapping out where to go, it helps to be clear about the current picture. You have a professionally built website at braidedbyagb.co.uk, an established set of nine services spanning braids, twists, locs and kids styles, an online booking system with deposit handling, and an active Instagram presence. You have already tested paid advertising on both Facebook and Instagram — which demonstrates commitment to growth. The results just haven't matched the effort yet.

The primary goal is not simply more bookings — it is specifically 10 returning clients weekly. That is a meaningful distinction. A returning client is worth far more than a new one: they cost less to acquire, book more confidently, spend more over time, and are your most powerful source of referrals. Everything in this strategy has been designed with that retention lens in mind.

📍 Your Biggest Opportunity: Location

  • Farnborough, Hampshire is underserved for specialist African braiding. Very few competitors rank on Google in your radius.
  • Neighbouring towns — Aldershot, Fleet, Camberley, Guildford — all represent untapped catchment area.
  • On Google, being first in a low-competition local niche is far more valuable than being 5th in London.

Why Facebook & Instagram Ads Under-Delivered

Social media ads work on an interruption model — your ad appears in a feed while someone is scrolling for entertainment. They were not actively looking for braiding. You pay for impressions from people who may have zero intent to book. Conversion rates are typically lower, and you need sustained spend to build retargeting audiences.

Google Ads operates on the opposite principle — intent. Someone searches "African hair braiding near me" or "knotless braids Farnborough" and your ad appears. They are already in buying mode. For a local service business, this is almost always more efficient per booking, especially on a tight budget.

Google Ads Strategy

Google Ads will be your primary paid acquisition channel. With under £100/month, the key is hyper-precision: the right keywords, tight geography, and ad copy that speaks directly to the person searching. Here is how to set it up correctly from day one.

2.1 Account & Campaign Setup

Step 1: Create Your Google Ads Account

Go to ads.google.com and sign in with a Google account (ideally the same one linked to your Google Business Profile). Choose "Create a campaign" → "Search" campaign type. Set the goal to "Leads" — this lets you track booking form completions as conversions.

Step 2: Geography & Language — The Most Critical Setting

With a small budget, wasting clicks on people in London or Birmingham is expensive. Be precise:

2.5 Landing Page Fixes

Every £1 spent on ads is only as effective as the page people land on. Here are the critical improvements needed on braidedbyagb.co.uk for ad traffic to convert.

⚠️ Critical Landing Page Improvements

  1. Add a prominent "Book Now" button above the fold on mobile — visible without scrolling.
  2. Display your phone/WhatsApp number in the top header on every page, not just the footer.
  3. Add a short sentence on the homepage mentioning Farnborough by name (Google uses this for geo-relevance).
  4. Ensure page load speed is under 3 seconds on mobile — use PageSpeed Insights (free) to check.
  5. Add a trust signal near the booking button: "5★ rated" or "Trusted by 200+ clients".
  6. Consider creating a dedicated landing page per service (e.g. /knotless-braids-farnborough) for higher ad relevance scores.

Retention Strategy

Getting to 10 returning clients per week cannot be achieved through ads alone — no matter the channel or budget. The most efficient path is to turn every new client into a repeat one. The following strategies work together to build a loyal client base that sustains your business independently of ad spend.

3.1 The Rebooking Conversation — Single Highest-Impact Action

Braiding services are naturally recurring. Box braids last 6–8 weeks, loc retwists every 4–6 weeks, cornrows often monthly. Most clients want to come back — they just forget to book. Close that gap at the chair.

💬 Script for the Chair

At the end of every appointment, say:

"Your braids will be looking their best for about 6–8 weeks. Would you like to book your next appointment now so you don't lose your slot?"

Then open your booking system and book them in before they leave. This single habit, done consistently, can add 3–5 returning clients per week on its own — with zero ad spend.

3.2 WhatsApp Rebooking Reminders

Send a short, warm message to each past client approximately 5 weeks after their appointment. Keep a simple note of appointment dates — your booking system already stores these.

Hi [Name]! It's [Your name] from BraidedbyAGB 💜

Hope you're loving your [style]! It's been about 5 weeks so just checking in — would you like to book your next appointment? I have slots coming up soon 😊

Reply here or book online: braidedbyagb.co.uk ✨

Send this to every client 35 days after their appointment. Even a 30% reply rate on 20 past clients = 6 bookings per month from a free channel.

3.3 Email Marketing

Your website already captures customer emails at booking. A monthly email to your client list is one of the highest-ROI marketing actions available to a small business.

PlatformMailchimp (free up to 500 contacts) or Brevo (free up to 300 emails/day)
FrequencyOnce per month maximum. More than that reduces open rates.
Best subject lines"Summer styles now booking 🌸", "New: Starter Locs — Begin your journey", "Bank holiday slots — book before they go"
Content formula1 featured service + 1 client photo (with permission) + 1 booking link + 1 care tip
Expected impactA 20% open rate on 100 contacts = 20 people who see your message. At 10% click rate = 10 potential bookings per send.

Other Recommended Channels

4.1 Google Business Profile — Do This Before Ads

If your Google Business Profile (GBP) is not fully optimised, do this first. GBP listings appear above paid ads in local search results and are completely free. This is the single highest-ROI action available to your business.

✅ Google Business Profile Checklist

  • Claim and verify your profile at business.google.com
  • Set primary category to "Hair Braiding Salon" + secondary: "Hair Salon"
  • Add all 9 services with descriptions and prices from your website
  • Upload at least 20 photos — before/afters, workspace, style close-ups
  • Add your booking link as the primary appointment URL
  • Post a "Google Post" every 2 weeks — these appear in search results
  • Ask every happy client to leave a Google Review — aim for 20+ reviews
  • Enable messaging so potential clients can contact you directly from Google Search

A fully optimised GBP with 20+ reviews consistently generates free bookings. Many local service businesses get 40–60% of their bookings from GBP alone.
