Your contact form and WhatsApp stay as they are, and messages from the form still reach your inbox. The address is also removed from the hidden business details on the homepage that only search engines read. Google doesn't need it to list your business, and your phone, WhatsApp and address stay.

What to expect

  • No change to how the site looks or works for clients.
  • New spam lists can't pick up your address from the website.
  • Lists that already have it will keep sending for a while, so spam drops off gradually rather than stopping overnight.
  • Keep marking spam as spam in your inbox, so the filter learns.
  • Your booking emails, receipts and replies are not affected.
