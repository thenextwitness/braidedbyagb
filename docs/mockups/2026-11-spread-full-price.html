How it works

  • Pay the deposit now works exactly as today (30% now, the rest on the day).
  • Spread the full price: Klarna or Clearpay pay you the full amount straight away; the client pays them back in instalments. Your booking is fully paid.
  • The instalment amounts shown are a guide; Klarna/Clearpay confirm the exact plan at their checkout.
  • Klarna and Clearpay each have their own limits and approval checks. If a client isn't approved, they can go back and pay the deposit instead.
  • Home-service travel fees are included in the spread amount.
  • PayPal: PayPal decides at its own checkout whether to offer the client "Pay in 3" (it depends on their PayPal account and the amount). If they aren't offered it, they simply pay the full price with PayPal, so the booking is still fully paid.

One thing to check (in your Stripe account)

Klarna, Clearpay and PayPal must be switched on in Stripe: Settings → Payment methods. I can't see your Stripe account, so if they're off, the "Spread the full price" option will be hidden automatically until you switch them on.
