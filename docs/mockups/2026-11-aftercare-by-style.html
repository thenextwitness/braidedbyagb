It was a joy doing your Knotless Braids. Thank you for trusting BraidedbyAGB with your hair.

A few tips to keep your braids looking fresh for longer:

  • Sleep in a satin bonnet or scarf, or on a satin pillowcase, to keep your braids neat and frizz-free.
  • Keep your scalp moisturised with a light oil or braid spray, focusing on the partings.
  • Cleanse your scalp every 1–2 weeks with diluted shampoo, then let your braids dry fully.
  • Avoid tight high buns or ponytails for the first few days.
  • Take them out within 6–8 weeks to protect your edges and natural hair.

To: Daniel, who had Traditional Loc Retwists

BraidedbyAGB

Thank you for coming in, Daniel! 💜

It was a joy doing your Traditional Loc Retwists. Thank you for trusting BraidedbyAGB with your hair.

A few tips to keep your locs looking fresh for longer:

  • Cover your locs with a satin bonnet or durag at night to keep lint out and your retwist neat.
  • Use light, water-based products. Heavy creams and wax build up inside locs.
  • Wash every 1–2 weeks with a residue-free shampoo, and dry your locs completely.
  • Try not to twist your roots between visits, as it can thin them.
  • Book your next retwist in 4–6 weeks.
