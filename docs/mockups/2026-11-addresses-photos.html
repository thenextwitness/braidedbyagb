About AI-generated photos

✕ Not recommended for showing your services. An AI photo shows hair you didn't do, and clients choose and pay based on what they see:

  • UK advertising rules (ASA / CAP code) say pictures of results must not mislead. A made-up "result" can count as misleading.
  • AI gets braids subtly wrong (parts, tension, hairlines). Braiders and clients notice, and it can make real work look less trustworthy.
  • A client who brings an AI picture as "the look I booked" may expect something that isn't achievable.

What I recommend instead

  • ✓ Now: use your Gallery photos automatically (above): 4 services covered today.
  • ✓ Quick win: your Instagram already shows your work. Save your best photo for each style and upload it in Admin → Services → edit → photo (about 1 minute each).
  • ✓ Ongoing: clients already tick "you can use photos of my hair" when booking. Snap a photo at the end of those appointments.
  • ✓ Until then, the purple BraidedbyAGB tile stays (it looks tidy and on-brand).
